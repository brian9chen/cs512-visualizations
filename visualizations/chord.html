Chord Key Lookup

Follow a key lookup around the ring and see how each finger table chooses the next hop.

The lookup rules and assumptions
  • Key ownership: successor(k) is the first active node at or clockwise after k, wrapping through zero.
  • Finger construction: finger i points to successor((n + 2i) mod 2m), with i = 0, …, m − 1.
  • Routing: if k ∈ (n, successor(n)], contact the successor. Otherwise, forward to the closest finger strictly preceding k clockwise.

Assumptions: a stable ring, correct finger tables, no failed nodes, and identifiers already hashed into a 6-bit space. The final hop contacts the owner; response messages are omitted. Algorithm reference: Stoica et al., Chord (SIGCOMM 2001).
